<h2 align="center">Sanchay</h2>

<p align="center">An open-source personal finance app and AI CFO for the Mac. Built for India, private by design.</p>

<p align="center">
  <a href="https://github.com/ashwinsingh2007/sanchay"><b>ashwinsingh2007/sanchay</b></a> · <a href="https://sanchayhq.github.io">Website</a> · <a href="https://github.com/ashwinsingh2007/sanchay/releases/latest">Download for Mac</a>
</p>

Sanchay reads your bank and credit card statements on your Mac, tracks your spending, and plans your savings and financial independence with an AI that runs locally through Ollama or in the cloud with your personal details masked. It has no server, no accounts and no telemetry. It is built by [Ashwani Kumar](https://github.com/ashwinsingh2007).

Contributions are welcome. Good places to start are the [good first issues](https://github.com/ashwinsingh2007/sanchay/issues?q=is%3Aissue+is%3Aopen+label%3A%22good+first+issue%22) and the [contributor guide](https://github.com/ashwinsingh2007/sanchay/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md).
